[h2]安装[/h2]

1. 先订阅 [b]BaseLib[/b]（版本 ≥ 3.4.7）。没有它本 Mod 不会加载，日志里也不会有任何 [code][Yetannia][/code] 字样。
2. 把本 Mod 解压到 [code]...\Slay the Spire 2\mods\[/code]，使结果是 [code]mods\Yetannia\Yetannia.dll[/code] 这样的结构。
3. 启动游戏，选人界面应能看到耶坦尼娅。

[h2]更新记录[/h2]

[b]v1.0.3[/b]
[list]
[*]修复[b]联机时「预见」会让卡牌卡在屏幕中间、整局动不了[/b]的问题。根因已经用真实联机日志定位：如果战斗结束的那一瞬间正好有一次预见在等玩家选牌，引擎会取消持有这次选择的那条操作，于是这次选择再也没人应答，等待就永远不返回——牌就一直冻在屏幕上。现在战斗只要结束或即将结束，这次预见会安全放弃，不再挂住整局。
[*]另有玩家反馈「预见时界面位置不对」，这一项[b]本轮也已修好[/b]：原因是引擎卡牌网格居中时用的是「一行的容量」（这里是 5 张）而不是实际显示的张数，所以只翻出 1 张时那张牌会被丢在 5 张宽框的最左边——1920 宽的屏幕上落在 x≈375，右边一大片空。现在改按实际张数居中（同一位置实测：1 张时从 x=375 移到 x=935，4 张时整体右移 140）。[b]只影响预见的选牌界面，其他卡牌网格不动。[/b]
[/list]

[h1]English[/h1]

[b]BETA BRANCH ONLY[/b] — this mod is developed and tested on the [b]beta branch[/b] of Slay the Spire 2 only. On the public (non-beta) branch it is not guaranteed to load: the character may not appear, card art may be missing, or event dialogue may throw errors. Please switch the game to the beta branch in Steam first.

[h2]What this mod adds[/h2]

[b]New character: Yetannia[/b]

[list]
[*][b]82 cards[/b] built around Scry, benchmarked against Slay the Spire 1 characters and stronger than Slay the Spire 2 ones. They complete the half-finished Scry framework the Watcher had in Slay the Spire 1, and every card was hand-designed by an author with over a thousand hours combined in Slay the Spire 1 and 2.
[*][b]The "Prophet" keyword[/b]: when the card moves from your draw pile to your discard pile, its Prophet effect triggers
[*][b]Starter relic "Prophet's Hair Ornament"[/b], and its upgraded form [b]"Prophet's Crown"[/b] (obtained through Orobas's upgrade replacement)
[*][b]11 voiced moments[/b]: attack, skill, taking fatal damage, character select, run start, combat start, card reward, campfire, shop, event, relic
[*][b]Dedicated dialogue for all nine Ancients[/b] (Neow / Orobas / Pael / Vakuu / Tanx / Tezcatara / Nonupeipe / Darv / The Architect)
[*][b]Animated[/b] figures in combat, at the campfire and in shops — not static portraits
[*]Its own art for the character-select screen, the energy orb, the map marker, and the card and relic icons
[/list]

[h2]Voice language[/h2]

The game's settings gain a [b]Voice Language[/b] option: [b]Chinese / Japanese / Off[/b].

"Off" silences only her voice — [b]the game's own sound effects are unaffected[/b].

[h2]Installation[/h2]

1. Subscribe to [b]BaseLib[/b] first (version ≥ 3.4.7). Without it this mod will not load, and there will be no [code][Yetannia][/code] lines in the log at all.
2. Extract this mod into [code]...\Slay the Spire 2\mods\[/code], so that you end up with [code]mods\Yetannia\Yetannia.dll[/code].
3. Launch the game — Yetannia should appear on the character-select screen.

[h2]Changelog[/h2]

[b]v1.0.3[/b]
[list]
[*]Fixed [b]a card freezing mid-screen and locking the whole run in multiplayer[/b]. The root cause was found in real multiplayer logs: if the combat ends at the exact moment a Scry is still waiting for its player choice, the engine cancels the action that owns that choice, so nothing ever answers it and the wait never returns — leaving the card frozen on screen forever. A Scry whose fight has ended (or is ending) is now abandoned safely instead of holding up the run.
[*]The separate report of "the Scry screen is in the wrong place" is [b]also fixed in this build[/b]: the engine's card grid centres using the row's CAPACITY (5 here) instead of how many cards are actually shown, so a scry revealing a single card drew it at the far left of a five-wide box (x≈375 on a 1920-wide screen, with most of the screen empty). The row is now centred on the real card count (measured: 1 card moves from x=375 to x=935; four cards shift right by 140). [b]Only the Scry selection screen is affected — other card grids are untouched.[/b]
[/list]
